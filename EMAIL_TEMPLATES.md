## 🎯 Email Best Practices

### Subject Lines That Get Opened:
✅ "Scalable Data Pipeline Architecture for [Company]"
✅ "[Specific Problem] Solution - Case Study Attached"
✅ "Introduction from [Mutual Contact]"
✅ "Quick Question About [Their Recent Post/News]"
❌ "Looking for work" (too generic)
❌ "Freelance Data Engineer Available" (spam-like)

### Email Structure:
1. **Hook** (first 2 lines) - Why you're reaching out
2. **Credibility** (3-5 bullets) - Relevant work & outcomes
3. **Value Proposition** (1 paragraph) - What you can do for them
4. **Call to Action** (1 line) - Clear next step
5. **Signature** (contact info + portfolio)

### Timing:
- **Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- **Best times:** 9-10 AM, 2-3 PM (their local time)
- **Follow-up:** After 3-5 business days
- **Give up:** After 3 follow-ups (move on)

### Personalization Checklist:
- [ ] Use their first name
- [ ] Reference specific company/product detail
- [ ] Mention relevant project from portfolio
- [ ] Ask specific question (not generic)
- [ ] Include one relevant link (not full portfolio dump)

### Response Rate Goals:
- **Cold email:** 5-10% response rate
- **Warm intro:** 30-50% response rate
- **Follow-up:** 10-15% additional responses
- **Aim for:** 2-3 conversations per 20 emails sent
